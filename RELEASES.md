# Releases

## 1.0.1 (2026-11-04)
- Fixed "Walk through parked horses" only working for the player who turned it on. In multiplayer,
  the host's setting now applies to every player.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-29)
- First release.
- The ridden horse's hitbox is narrowed from 96 px to 48 px (the farmer's width), so the horse fits
  through one-tile gaps.
- New setting "Walk through parked horses" (off by default), available in Generic Mod Config Menu
  or `config.json`.
